What the app provides

Shop Registration

Register a new FPS shop and complete the required activation payment through Razorpay.

Reports

Access daily, monthly and custom AePDS shop reports from the Android application.

Secure Payments

Payments are processed through Razorpay Checkout and verified by the application server.

Registration & Payment

New shop registration includes a ₹59 activation payment. The payment is initiated through Razorpay Checkout. Access is activated only after successful server-side payment verification.

Terms & Conditions

The application is provided for FPS shop reporting and related account-management purposes. Users are responsible for providing accurate registration information and using the application only for legitimate business purposes.

Privacy Policy

The application uses information required for account registration, authentication, shop identification and payment processing. Payment credentials are handled by Razorpay; the application does not request or store card PINs, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

If a payment is not successfully captured, the payment may be automatically refunded according to the payment provider's processing rules. For registration or activation issues after a captured payment, users should contact support with the transaction details.
