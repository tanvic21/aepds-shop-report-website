How Registration and Activation Work

The registration and activation process uses server-side payment verification before access is activated.

1. Register

Enter the required shop and account details in the Android application.

2. Pay the Displayed Fee

Review the one-time ₹59 registration and software activation fee and complete payment using the payment option available in the app.

3. Verification and Access

The server verifies the payment. Account activation proceeds after successful verification, allowing the user to log in and access supported reports.
