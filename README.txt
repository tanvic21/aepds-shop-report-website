Before publishing/submitting to Razorpay:
1. Add the real business/support email or phone number in index.html.
2. Review Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Refund & Cancellation Policy with the actual business terms.
3. Publish the site publicly over HTTPS.
4. Use the resulting public HTTPS URL in Razorpay's Add your app link/website verification form.
